# Revision analyses: results summary (run of 2026-11-10)

Every number below is read from a CSV written in this run under `revision_results/` (source named on each table).
The tables are generated by `scripts/rev_tables.py` (→ `summary_tables.md`, pasted verbatim below). AUC/AP/Brier
2 decimals, p 3 decimals (ECE and Brier at 3 to separate close values). The manuscript, `results/` and
the labelling/zones/windows/features/architectures/hyperparameters/epochs/seeds/LOTO protocol were not changed.

## What changed in the conclusions

The benchmark reproduces exactly: retrained LR, RF, SVM, BiLSTM, fusion MLP and ST-GAT give probabilities identical
to `results/predictions.csv` (max |Δp| = 0) and the same pooled AUCs (0.69, 0.74, 0.65, 0.73, 0.71, 0.69).
**The chatter indicator (CI) is not what the models use.** Removing the six CI columns (F1) changes pooled AUC by
−0.02 to +0.01 across all six models (all Holm p = 1.000), each CI channel alone reaches only 0.56–0.61 (sign chosen
post hoc, zone-permutation p ≥ 0.213), and grouped permutation importance of CI is ≈ 0 or negative. Statistical-only
features (F3) match the full set (LR 0.73, RF 0.74, fusion 0.73, BiLSTM 0.70, ST-GAT 0.69); the strongest single
features are crest factor, kurtosis and band centroid (0.80–0.83) and force mode-2 energy (0.73–0.77). Removing the
force chatter-band features (F2) lowers every model (−0.02 to −0.07), but no ablation survives Holm correction
(smallest Holm p 0.094, fusion MLP F2). **The significance claims weaken once trials are treated as the unit.** With
the manuscript's zone-level null, 7/9 Table 3 models have p < 0.05 (recomputed here; SVM and 1D-CNN do not) but only RF survives Holm
(manuscript p: Holm 0.018; recomputed: 0.040). Under a trial-block null (scores fixed) only RF (0.023), BiLSTM (0.021)
and fusion MLP (0.047) have raw p < 0.05 and none survives Holm (smallest 0.193). Under full retraining with
trial-block-permuted labels, LR is not significant (F0 p = 0.146 two-sided, 0.052 one-sided; F1 0.124 / 0.042);
RF is not significant two-sided either (188 permutations: F0 p = 0.106, one-sided 0.037; F1 0.095 / 0.032). None of the 18 Table 4 comparisons is significant raw or after Holm. **Discrimination is mainly
between trials, not between zones within a trial.** Trial-level AUC (any unstable zone) is 0.77–0.90 for all models
except the 1D-CNN (0.46), but in the 8 trials with mixed labels the mean within-trial AUC is 0.50–0.62 for every
model except LR at F0 (0.81) and the 1D-CNN (0.44), and within-trial permutation nulls are centred on the observed
AUC (one-sided p ≥ 0.169). Dropping trials 04 and 06 (post hoc, no retraining) lowers pooled AUC to 0.61–0.69 at F0 (1D-CNN 0.52).
**Probabilities are poorly calibrated** except RF: Brier skill vs the constant 0.52 predictor is 0.14 (RF) and 0.07
(SVM), and negative for LR (−0.05), BiLSTM (−0.06), fusion MLP (−0.07) and ST-GAT (−0.18), with calibration slopes
0.16–0.22 (over-confident). Deep-model probabilities also depend strongly on the seed (largest per-zone seed range
0.70–0.99 for BiLSTM, fusion MLP, ST-GAT and ST-GCN, 0.56 for the small GNN, vs 0 for LR, 0.07–0.12 for SVM
and 0.09–0.14 for RF; `seed_spread.csv`).

## Definitions and caveats needed to read the tables

* **Original permutation test (Table 3, `src/evaluate.py:summarise`).** It permutes the 48 pooled zone labels
  (`rng.permutation(y)`, one `default_rng(0)` used sequentially across models) with the out-of-fold scores held fixed;
  two-sided p = (1 + #{|AUC_null − 0.5| ≥ |AUC − 0.5|}) / (N + 1), N = 2000. It ignores that zones are nested in trials.
* **(a) Trial-block null:** the 16 trials' 3-zone label vectors are permuted between trials (scores fixed, 2000
  permutations, `default_rng(0)` fresh per model). **(b) Within-trial null:** labels shuffled within each trial (scores
  fixed, 2000); it keeps the between-trial structure, so it tests only within-trial ranking (one-sided p reported).
  **(c) Retraining null:** labels permuted as trial blocks, then the full LOTO is retrained;
  permutation b uses `default_rng([0, b])`; LR uses one seed (42) per permutation because LR probabilities are
  identical across seeds (`seed_spread.csv`: 0), RF uses all 5 seeds. The observed labelling (perm = −1) was
  re-fitted inside the same runner and asserted equal to the benchmark AUC.
* **Statistics RNG.** Bootstrap CIs and permutation nulls here use a fresh `default_rng(0)` per model/config, whereas
  the manuscript used one generator sequentially; AUCs are identical but CIs and p differ in the third decimal
  (compare the two zone-level Holm families).
* **Step 1 feature sets** (`PIPELINE_MAP.md` has the 62-column map): F0 all 62; F1 −CI (6 cols); F2 −force
  chatter-band features (mode1, mode2, CI, band centroid of Fx, Fy, Fz; 12 cols); F3 statistical (rms, p2p, kurtosis,
  crest) + spindle speed + zone position; F4 spectral only; F5 spectral without CI; F6 force + kinematic; F7 accel. +
  kinematic; F8 accel. without CI + kinematic. Columns are dropped before standardisation. **The fusion MLP and ST-GAT
  also receive the raw force and acceleration windows through their CNN encoders, which no feature configuration
  touches**; their ablations therefore remove only engineered features. **The 1D-CNN uses only raw windows and is
  unaffected by feature ablation** (not re-run). Small GNN and ST-GCN were run at F1 only (optional); their F0
  was not retrained here, so no paired ΔAUC is given for them.
* **Single-feature AUC:** the sign (direction) of each feature was chosen post hoc to give AUC ≥ 0.5 and is stated;
  these AUCs are therefore optimistic.
* **Step 4:** within-trial AUC is defined only for the 8 trials whose three zones contain both classes, each with
  2–3 zones, so each per-trial AUC is 0, 0.5 or 1. The "without 04, 06" AUC is the same out-of-fold predictions with
  those trials removed (no retraining). Trial-level AUC uses the mean zone score per trial; its CI is a trial bootstrap.
* **Step 5:** ECE uses 5 equal-count bins (n = 48 gives 9–10 zones per bin); the constant-predictor Brier uses the
  in-sample prevalence (25/48 = 0.52). SVM probabilities come from libsvm's internal Platt scaling (5-fold CV on the
  training trials); LR, RF and deep models are not recalibrated. Calibration slope/intercept from logistic
  recalibration on logit(p) (statsmodels); CITL = intercept with logit(p) as offset.
* **Permutation importance (LR, RF at F0; `importance.csv`):** each LOTO fold model is fitted exactly as in the
  benchmark and scores its held-out trial with one feature (or group) permuted. "Across" draws the values from all 48
  held-out zones; "within-trial" permutes them only among the 3 zones of the held-out trial, so it removes only
  within-trial information. ΔAUC is computed on the pooled, seed-averaged OOF scores (per-fold AUC is undefined in
  8/16 single-class folds); 20 repeats, 5 seeds, `default_rng(0)`.
